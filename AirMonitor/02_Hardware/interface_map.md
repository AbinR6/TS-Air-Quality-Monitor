# Hardware Interface & Bus Map

This interface map defines every physical bus, serial protocol, signal direction, voltage level, and connector allocation across the Air Monitor hardware.

## 3. Bus Allocations & Contention Safeguards
1. **SPI Bus Isolation**: The display operates at 40 MHz SPI on dedicated pins. No external devices share this bus to guarantee flicker-free pixel streaming.
2. **I2C Addressing Map**:
   - `0x44`: Sensirion SHT41 (Temperature & Humidity)
   - `0x62`: Sensirion SCD41 (Carbon Dioxide)
   - `0x15`: CST816S Capacitive Touch Controller
   - All addresses are unique and operate harmoniously at 400 kHz Fast-Mode.
